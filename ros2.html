Node groups

What each part does.

Acquisition

hamr_uros_bridge
Exchanges motor rates, encoder counts, and IMU packets with the drive ESP.
caster_encoder_bridge
Receives H1, H2, and CENTER telemetry over Wi-Fi; retains timing and validity.
d455_camera · d455_imu_filter
Publishes RGB-D observations and an optional filtered camera IMU.

Localization

holonomic_odom_node
Converts wheel and turret counts into motion estimates.
ekf_filter_node
Fuses wheel motion and the chassis IMU into continuous local odometry.
rtabmap · rgbd_odometry
Builds or revisits a visual map; RGB-D odometry is an optional source.

Motion

continuous_waypoint
Blends corners and limits speed, acceleration, and jerk.
map_reference_to_odom
Expresses map-frame references in the controller's local frame.
hamr_controller_node
Tracks position and heading, then computes wheel and turret rates.

Simulation

parameter_bridge
Connects Gazebo state and actuator topics to ROS 2.
compa_controller_node
Runs the simulation chassis controller, including gimbal axes.
robot_state_publisher
Publishes the robot's link transforms from joint positions.

Terrain planning

terrain_map_publisher
Publishes occupancy, height, and elevation layers.
astar_on_costmap
Finds an off-road route subject to slope and step limits.
Nav2 servers Target
Plan and follow obstacle-aware routes using the mapped environment.

Experiment tools

rosbag2 · record_hamr_test_bag
Stores commands and measurements for paired analysis.
replay_caster_encoder_joints
Reconstructs caster joints from recorded telemetry.
foxglove_bridge · rviz2
Displays live topics, trajectories, transforms, and recorded motion.
